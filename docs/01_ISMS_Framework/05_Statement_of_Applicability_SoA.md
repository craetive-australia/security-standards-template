## 1. Purpose *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 6.1.3d, SOC 2 CC5.3, NIST CSF 2.0 GV.PO-01)*
The Statement of Applicability (SoA) is a mandatory, core governance artifact of [COMPANY_NAME]'s Information Security Management System (ISMS). In accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 6.1.3(d), this document records the applicability of all 93 controls from Annex A, provides justification for their inclusion or exclusion, documents current implementation statuses, establishes crosswalk mappings to legacy ISO/IEC 27001:2013 controls, AICPA SOC 2 (2017/2022) Trust Services Criteria, and NIST CSF 2.0, and provides direct relative links to implementing policies and operational standards.

## 2. Methodology & Scope
All 93 controls from ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A have been evaluated based on the results of [COMPANY_NAME]'s formal Information Security Risk Assessment and contractual obligations. Every control has been deemed applicable ('Yes') to ensure defense-in-depth, robust multi-cloud security, supply chain resilience, and compliance with global privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA).

The 93 controls are structured across four organizational themes:
- **Clause 5: Organizational controls** (Controls A.5.1 through A.5.37)
- **Clause 6: People controls** (Controls A.6.1 through A.6.8)
- **Clause 7: Physical controls** (Controls A.7.1 through A.7.14)
- **Clause 8: Technological controls** (Controls A.8.1 through A.8.34)
