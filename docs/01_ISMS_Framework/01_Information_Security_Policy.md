## 1. Purpose and Scope *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 5.2, SOC 2 CC1.1, CC1.2, NIST CSF 2.0 GV.PO-01)*
The purpose of this Information Security Policy is to define the framework for protecting [COMPANY_NAME]'s information assets against all internal, external, deliberate, or accidental threats. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, and third parties working on behalf of [COMPANY_NAME].

## 2. Information Security Objectives *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 6.2, NIST CSF 2.0 GV.PO-01)*
[COMPANY_NAME] is committed to preserving the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of its information assets. Our primary objectives are to:
- Comply with all applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.
- Maintain a robust Information Security Management System (ISMS) to identify and mitigate risks.
- Ensure business continuity and minimize the impact of security incidents.
- Foster a culture of security awareness among all personnel.

## 3. Management Commitment *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 5.1, NIST CSF 2.0 GV.RR-01)*
Executive management at [COMPANY_NAME] is fully committed to the ISMS. This includes:
- Providing the necessary resources to establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve the ISMS.
- Communicating the importance of effective information security management.
- Ensuring the ISMS achieves its intended outcomes.

## 4. Policy Statements
### 4.1 Access Control *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.15, A.5.16, A.5.18, A.8.2, SOC 2 CC6.1, NIST CSF 2.0 PR.AA-01)*
Access to [COMPANY_NAME]'s information systems must be granted based on the principle of least privilege and need-to-know.

### 4.2 Asset Management *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.9, A.5.10, A.5.12, SOC 2 CC6.1, NIST CSF 2.0 ID.AM-01, PR.DS-01)*
All information assets must be identified, classified, and protected according to their criticality and sensitivity.

### 4.3 Incident Management *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.24, A.5.25, A.5.26, A.5.27, A.5.28, SOC 2 CC7.3, NIST CSF 2.0 RS.MA-01)*
All security events and incidents must be reported promptly to the security team and managed according to the Incident Response Plan.

## 5. Violations
Any violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract, and potential legal action.

## 6. Review and Update
This policy shall be reviewed at least annually or when significant changes occur to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness.
