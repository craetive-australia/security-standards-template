## 1. Purpose
The Business Continuity Plan (BCP) outlines the procedures and resources required to ensure that [COMPANY_NAME]'s essential business functions can continue to operate during and after a significant disruption.
*(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.29, A.5.30, SOC 2 CC9.1, NIST CSF 2.0 RC.RP-01)*

## 2. Scope
This plan covers all critical business processes, personnel, and facilities required to maintain minimum viable operations. It complements the Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP), which focuses specifically on IT systems.

## 3. Business Impact Analysis (BIA) Summary

Based on the most recent BIA, the following are identified as critical business functions, along with their Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD):
*   **[Critical Function 1, e.g., Customer Support]:** MTD - [4 hours]
*   **[Critical Function 2, e.g., Order Processing]:** MTD - [12 hours]
*   **[Critical Function 3, e.g., Payroll]:** MTD - [3 days]

## 4. Continuity Strategies

To maintain operations during a disruption, [COMPANY_NAME] relies on the following strategies:
*   **Alternate Facilities:** In the event the primary office is inaccessible, employees will transition to a remote work model (Work From Home) utilizing secure VPN access.
*   **Redundant Personnel:** Critical roles have designated alternates cross-trained to perform essential duties.
*   **Supplier Contingency:** Secondary vendors are identified for critical services (e.g., alternate ISPs, secondary logistics providers).

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
*   **Crisis Management Team (CMT):** Led by the [CEO / Executive Director]. Responsible for strategic decision-making, declaring a disaster, and coordinating overall response.
*   **Department Leads:** Responsible for executing continuity procedures for their specific functional areas and accounting for staff.

## 6. Activation and Procedures

### 6.1 Initial Assessment
Upon notification of a significant event (e.g., natural disaster, prolonged power outage, pandemic), the CMT will convene to assess the impact on operations.

### 6.2 Plan Activation
If the disruption is expected to exceed acceptable downtime limits, the CMT will formally declare a disaster and activate this BCP.

### 6.3 Operational Procedures
*   **Communication:** Employees will be notified via [Emergency Alert System / Phone Tree / Slack] regarding the situation and their required actions.
*   **Relocation/Remote Work:** Staff required for critical functions will initiate remote work protocols.
*   **Manual Workarounds:** If IT systems are unavailable, departments will switch to documented manual workarounds (e.g., paper ledgers, offline forms) as outlined in departmental specific annexes.

## 7. Plan Maintenance and Testing
*   This BCP must be reviewed and updated at least [annually] or upon significant organizational changes.
*   A tabletop exercise or functional test of this plan must be conducted at least [annually] to ensure its effectiveness.
