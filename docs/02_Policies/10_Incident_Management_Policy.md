## 1. Purpose
To ensure a rapid, automated, and effective approach to the management of information security incidents, minimizing downtime and data exposure. *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.24, SOC 2 CC7.3, NIST CSF 2.0 RS.MA-01)*

## 2. Scope
Applies to all security events, breaches, and vulnerabilities affecting [COMPANY_NAME]'s infrastructure, products, and employee endpoints.

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 Alerting and Reporting
* **Automated Routing:** Security alerts generated by SIEM, EDR, or cloud security posture management (CSPM) tools must automatically route to on-call security engineers via an alerting platform (e.g., PagerDuty, Opsgenie).
* **SLAs:** Critical (Sev1) incidents demand a maximum 15-minute acknowledgment time from the on-call responder, 24/7/365. *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.25, SOC 2 CC7.3, NIST CSF 2.0 RS.AN-03)*

### 3.2 Incident Response Process and Automation
* **SOAR Integration:** Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response (SOAR) playbooks must be utilized to automatically isolate compromised endpoints or block malicious IPs upon detection.
* **The Response Lifecycle:** 
  1. **Identification:** Validation via SIEM logs and threat intel.
  2. **Containment:** Network isolation via EDR or firewall rules.
  3. **Eradication:** Wiping affected instances, rotating compromised credentials.
  4. **Recovery:** Restoring from immutable backups.
  *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.26, SOC 2 CC7.3, NIST CSF 2.0 RS.RP-01)*

### 3.3 Blameless Post-Mortems and SLAs
* **Root Cause Analysis:** A formal, blameless post-mortem document must be completed within **5 business days** for all Sev1 and Sev2 (critical and high) incidents, and within **10 business days** for Sev3 incidents. Post-mortem action items must result in prioritized engineering Jira tickets to prevent recurrence. *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.27, SOC 2 CC7.3, NIST CSF 2.0 RS.AN-04)*

### 3.4 Evidence Collection and Forensics
* **Chain of Custody:** EDR tools must be configured to support remote memory dumps and forensic artifact collection. Immutable audit logs must be preserved for investigative purposes. *(ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.28, SOC 2 CC7.4, NIST CSF 2.0 RS.AN-05)*

### 3.5 Out-of-Band Communication
* **Dedicated Channels:** Incident coordination must occur over dedicated, out-of-band communication channels (e.g., dedicated private Slack channels, encrypted conference bridges) to prevent adversary eavesdropping. *(SOC 2 CC7.4, NIST CSF 2.0 RS.CO-02)*

### 3.6 Statutory Breach Notification
* **Jurisdictional Compliance:** Where personal data or material operations are impacted, statutory notifications must be executed in accordance with applicable global timelines (e.g., GDPR 72 hours, UK GDPR 72 hours, NIS2 24h/72h, CIRCIA 72h/24h, SEC Form 8-K 4 business days) as codified in the [Data Breach Notification Procedure](../03_Standards_and_Procedures/09_Data_Breach_Notification_Procedure.md).

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Incident Commander (IC):** Drives technical incident triage, coordinates responder resources, and commands the war room.
- **[CISO_NAME]:** Serves as executive authority on public breach declarations, board notifications, and regulatory escalations.
- **Security Operations Engineers:** Execute containment playbooks, forensic data gathering, and system eradication.
- **Legal Counsel / DPO:** Evaluates jurisdictional reporting triggers and directs regulatory communications.
- **All Employees:** Required to immediately report suspicious activities, phishing emails, or security anomalies to the Security team.

## 5. Compliance and Exceptions
- **Compliance:** Immediate reporting of suspected security incidents is mandatory for all personnel. Concealing or intentionally delaying the reporting of a security incident constitutes grounds for disciplinary termination.
- **Exceptions:** No exceptions may be granted that bypass statutory breach reporting or executive incident notification. Operational triage variations must be approved in writing by the Incident Commander and documented in the [Security Incident Log](../04_Templates_and_Registers/03_Security_Incident_Log.md).
